## Sounds

- `sounds/sutras/*.m4a`, the 29 Sutra name clips: made with the open Kokoro-82M speech model (Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) through kokoro-js, from phonemes this repo generates from the IAST (`tools/sutra-voice/`). Voice: a blend of Kokoro's Hindi voice Alpha (70%) and Japanese voice Alpha (30%). Released with this repository under the MIT License.
- `sounds/om.m4a`, the chanted Om: "Om.ogg" by Tito Dutta, Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Om.ogg), CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication. Converted to AAC. It is atmosphere added by this app, not part of the 1965 book.
