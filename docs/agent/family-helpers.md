# The Nikhilam Family helpers

The decisions are in `openspec/changes/family-helpers/design.md` (D1 to D17, owner-approved 2026-10-21); the words are in `GLOSSARY.md` (Family helper, Shunya, Nava, Eka, Visit, Help signal).

- **Who.** Shunya (the Zero, funny), Nava (the Nine, witty) and Eka (the One, names the Sutra) are the Player's fellow students, never teachers and never pets; Bolt stays the teacher and they credit him ("Bolt showed us this one"). They give a hint, the method or a worked example on other numbers, never the answer to the Player's question.
- **The switch.** Off for everyone until a Player turns on "Try the Nikhilam Family helpers" in Me (`player.family`, `FAMILY_DEFAULT = false` in `js/family-helpers.js`); then "When they help" (`player.familyHelp`: `always`, `ask`, `off`). `js/family-hook.js` reads both and loads `js/family-view.js` only when they are on, so a Player who left the switch off loads nothing more. Releasing the Family is task 3.9: flip the default and remove the try switch.
- **Modules.** `js/family-helpers.js` is pure (no DOM, storage or timers): `createCoach()` (the help signals and escalation), `planVisit()` (the turns), `placeVisit()` (where a Visit stands), `exampleFor()` (other numbers), `memoryAfter()`; `js/family-lines.js` is the line table; `js/family-view.js` draws one Visit at a time on `.fh-layer` (z `--vm-z-family`, above the toasts) and times idle; `js/family.js` has `friendSVG(id)`, each friend cut from the brand drawing. Unit tests: `test/family-helpers.test.mjs`, `test/family-lines.test.mjs`, `test/brand.test.mjs`.
- **Round events.** A view calls `familyRound({ root, card, signal, area, protect, ctx, hint })` and sends `note({ type })` for `question`, `miss`, `right` (with `first`), `hint`, `leave`, and `raceStart` then `roundEnd({ misses, lit })` for a timed round (`raceEnd` when it is left before its end, so nothing held carries over); `idle` is timed by the overlay. Practice and Katas report live; the Star round reports only its end (`runStar`'s `onEnd`), so nothing appears mid-race. `protect` is the selector of what a Visit may never cover.
- **Rules in `RULES`.** 2 misses in a row on a Skill, 3 in a round, about 20 s idle, Hint twice, leaving after a miss; at most one automatic Visit every 3 minutes; Not now mutes for the browser session (`sessionStorage` `vm.familyMute`); the first Visit of a session greets by Nickname (`vm.familyHi`). Escalation 1, 2, 3 friends per Skill per session; a breakthrough cheer is all three.
- **Lines.** `LINES[friend][kind][band]`, bands `early`, `growing`, `fluent` (from `player.reading` when `age-bands` lands, else the Mode). No em dashes, no digits, Early at most 8 words a sentence and 2 sentences, Growing at most 14, placeholders only `{name}`, `{title}`, `{idea}`, `{sutra}`, `{meaning}`, `{wrong}`. `{name}` is filled as text by the overlay, never into a markup string. The lines are text only until the owner's recording lands (`docs/agent/studio-voice.md`); the task that wires their voices records them in the same PR.
- **Never sent.** `player.familyMem` (the hard Sutra or Skill) stays in `vm.players` on the device; the hand-off carries only known Player fields. Analytics are fixed words only: `math/family/visit/1..3`, `cheer`, `ask`, `show_me`, `not_now` (`metrics.family`).
- **Checks.** `e2e/tests/family-helpers.spec.ts` for the behaviors, `e2e/tests/cov/family-helpers-1..12`, `calm-3-family`, `analytics-12-family`, `star-round-17-family` for the scenarios (shared steps in `e2e/tests/family-kit.ts`). The prototype `docs/prototypes/family-helpers/` runs the real modules from a local server at the repository root; the voice sample tool `tools/voice/family-samples.mjs` is owner-run and spends only with `GO=1`.
