# Play experiences (`js/play/`)

- Twelve experiences (Sutra Spellbook, Discovery Locks, Party Mode, Ghost Races, Daily Number, Tala Lab, Mandala Studio, Pattern Wall, Teach the Apprentice, Estimation Bets, Math Lens, The Lost Śulba Scroll), each a page inside a section at `#/<section>/<name>`, listed in `js/play/catalog.js`. There is no separate play area or nav item; section pages show them on a shelf (`playShelf()` in `js/app.js`). Party Mode replaced the Duel (`#/duel` redirects).
- An experience is `js/play/exp/<id>.js` exporting `mount({ root, signal, params, exp })`, pure logic in `js/play/exp/<id>-*.js` (no DOM at import, tested in `test/play/<id>.test.mjs`), and `css/play/<id>.css` plus the shared `css/play/base.css`, loaded with the page (`build.py` stamps and precaches them through the `#vm-css` map). Class prefix `px-` (the video player owns `vp-`).
- Experiences use `js/play/kit.js`, never app modules: it reads the Mode, `vm.sound` and Calm from storage, keeps state per Player (`vm.play.<player id>.<key>`), and `track(action)` sends only `math/play/<experience>/<action>` and fires `vmplay`, which `onPlay()` in `js/app.js` turns into Sparks (at most 150 a day, each award and the cap said in a toast) and badge counts. Listeners, timers and frame loops go through `js/play/life.js` (the app's `js/life.js`). Where an experience holds input for feedback (a Discovery Locks miss), `hold(el, ms, then, { skippable, signal })` from the kit (the app's `js/hold.js`) replaces `later()`: a bar under the held keys after 1.2 s, and Next from 2 s when the round is untimed (openspec change `muse-review-polish`).
- Experiences post nothing to the leaderboards. Share links carry only parameters, times and word-list names.
- Math Lens keeps camera frames on the device. Its digit reader is Tesseract.js 5.1.1, served from this site (`vendor/tesseract/`, byte for byte from npm, hashes pinned by `test/play/lens-reader.test.mjs`), loaded on first use and never precached. The worker starts from its URL (`workerBlobURL: false`, so `worker-src 'self'` allows it) and compiles WebAssembly, which is why the published CSP has `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` in `script-src`; no other source is allowed and jsDelivr is not listed. The camera is offered only where the page's policy lets the reader run (`readerAllowed()` in `js/play/exp/lens-cam.js`). Camera and microphone stop on a route change, a hidden tab and errors.
- Dated and shareable state (Daily Number, ghosts, challenges) is seeded with versioned seeds and encoded in the URL hash; there is no server.
