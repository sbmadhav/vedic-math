Learn Vedic Math (learnvedicmath.com) is a math game for ages 4 to adult. We built it so that a child can play without telling us anything about themselves. This page says, in plain words, what we keep, what leaves your device, who gets it and why, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

## The short version (for kids)

- Your progress stays on this device. We do not ask for your name, your email or your birthday.
- Your nickname is made up for you, like "Swift Otter 42". Nobody types it.
- If you are under 13, nothing you do is posted online. Your scores go on this device's board only.
- If you are 13 or older, your made-up nickname, its color and your score can go on a World board that other players see.
- When you press play on a video, it comes from YouTube. Before you press play, YouTube is not told anything.
- Questions? A grown-up can write to privacy@learnvedicmath.com.

## What stays on your device

The game saves these in your browser's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not sent to us:

- each Player on the device: the made-up Nickname, an avatar color, the Mode (Explorer, Challenger or Pro), when the Player was added, and the answer to the one age question (a number of years, or "adult");
- progress: Skills practised, Ratings, Sparks, Medals, Stars, Belts, Badges, days played, best results;
- the Device board (scores of the Players on this device);
- settings: color scheme, read aloud, voice, music, Calm, Pause motion, text size, whether the tour was shown;
- if a Player 13 or older has posted to a World board: the random ID used for that (see below).

Nothing a Player types into an answer box is ever stored or sent.

To delete it: remove a Player on the Me page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Clearing site data deletes everything for every Player on the device.

## What leaves your device, to whom, and why

| What | Who receives it | Why | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your device's IP address, browser type and the page asked for | Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the site | To send you the pages and protect the site from attacks | Every visit |
| An anonymous page-load count (page, referring site, browser type, country, page speed) | Cloudflare Web Analytics | To know how many people use the site and whether it is fast | Each page load, unless your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control |

| Video request (your IP address, browser data, and what YouTube collects while the video plays) | Google LLC (YouTube), in privacy-enhanced mode | To play the video you chose | Only after you press play on a video |
| For Players 13 and older who post: the made-up Nickname, avatar color, score, the time, an expiry date, and a random ID | Google LLC (Firebase, Firestore) | To show the World board | When such a Player finishes a Ranked round, or the weekly Sparks total is posted |
| For Players 13 and older who open a World board or post: device and browser signals checked by reCAPTCHA | Google LLC (Firebase App Check, reCAPTCHA Enterprise) | To make sure posts come from our site and not from a cheating script | When such a Player opens a World board or posts |

None of the analytics tools sets a cookie or stores anything in your browser. None of them gets a Nickname, an age answer, a score, a typed answer or any ID of a Player.

## What we never do

- We never ask a child for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location, school or birthday.
- We never show ads, and never sell or rent any information.
- We never build a profile of a Player, and never use any information to contact a specific person.
- We never use Google Analytics or any other advertising or tracking tool.
- We never let a Player type anything that is posted or shown to others.

## Children under 13 (notice to parents)

We designed the site so that we do not collect personal information from children under 13.

- **Posting:** if a Player's age answer is under 13, the game never posts anything for them to any World board, in any Mode. Their scores rank only on the Device board. No random ID is ever created for them.
- **What is still processed when a child uses the site:** like every website, our host (Cloudflare) receives the device's IP address to deliver the pages. Our analytics count visits without cookies, without storing the IP address and without any ID. We rely on the COPPA exception for **support for the internal operations of the site** for this. We use this information only to deliver the site, keep it secure, and understand in aggregate how it is used and how fast it is. We make sure it cannot be used to contact a specific child or to build a profile: no cookies or device IDs are set, the IP address is not stored by our analytics, analytics events are made only of fixed words, nothing is shared for advertising, and the counts are only ever looked at in total.
- **Videos:** a video comes from YouTube (Google) only after a child presses play. YouTube then receives the device's IP address and browser data under its own rules ([YouTube's privacy notice](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). We show videos in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, we self-designate the site as directed to children where Google lets us, and we show the video's picture from our own site so that nothing reaches YouTube before play.
- **Can children make information public?** No. Players under 13 cannot post to World boards.
- **Your rights as a parent:** you can review what is kept about your child (it is on your device: open the Me page), delete it (remove the Player, or clear site data), and refuse further collection (there is no online collection for a child to stop; you can also turn off analytics with your browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track setting). If you think we hold anything about your child, write to privacy@learnvedicmath.com and we will answer within [30] days.
- **Parental consent:** because we do not collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what the internal-operations exception allows, we do not ask for parental consent. If that ever changes, for example with a paid family plan, we will ask a parent for verifiable consent first and tell you what changes.

## Teens and adults (13 and older)

A Player whose age answer is 13 or over can post to World boards. Each post holds only the made-up Nickname, an avatar color, the score, the time and an expiry date, filed under a random ID that belongs to that Player on that browser. Anyone can see the top 20 entries of a board: the Nickname, color, score and time. The random ID is created at that Player's first post, never before. Removing the Player on the Me page forgets the ID on the device; entries already posted delete themselves after 30 days, or sooner if you ask.

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| World board entries (Players 13 and older) | Deleted automatically 30 days after their last update; boards older than 35 days cannot be read |

## For the reviewing lawyer (remove before publishing)

Questions this draft depends on:

1. Is the site "directed to children" with children as its **primary audience**, or a **mixed audience** site under the amended Rule (§312.2)? If primary, every visitor must be treated as a child, and World boards for teens would need a different design (ADR 0006, considered options).
2. Does posting by self-declared 13+ Players, after a neutral age screen, satisfy the mixed-audience age-screen conditions? Is the age question's design (neutral, no default, asked at the first would-be post) adequate?
3. Are Cloudflare hosting logs, Cloudflare Web Analytics and GoatCounter correctly treated as support for internal operations (§312.5(c)(7)), and is the §312.4(d)(3) disclosure above sufficient?
4. YouTube: is the click-to-play facade plus privacy-enhanced mode plus self-designation enough, and must YouTube be named as a third party "to whom personal information is disclosed"? Is showing self-hosted copies of video thumbnails acceptable under copyright, or should the site draw its own title cards?
5. reCAPTCHA Enterprise for 13+ Players only: acceptable as a security internal operation for teens; any state law issue for 13 to 17?
6. Does moving to a for-profit business entity make it a "new operator" requiring a fresh notice to parents (§312.5(a)(1) material change)? The hand-off carries the age answer but not the old Parent step.
7. State laws for minors 13 to 17 (for example New York's Child Data Protection Act, Connecticut, Colorado, California AADC as enjoined in part).
8. Operator details: is a PO box or registered agent address acceptable in the notice?
9. Email Routing to a personal Gmail: acceptable, or should privacy@ be a business mailbox before launch?
10. Safe harbor program (kidSAFE, PRIVO, CARU) before any paid plan?
11. School use: teacher or school authorization, FERPA and state student-privacy laws before any classroom marketing.
12. The 2026 licence change: already-published versions were MIT, including the learning content. Confirm the MIT grant for those copies cannot be withdrawn, and how to word the notice for new content.
