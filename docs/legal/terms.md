## 1. What the site is

A free math learning game: Tricks from Bharati Krishna Tirtha's 1965 book, practice, games, videos chosen by us, and Boards. We may add, change or remove features. Some features may one day be part of a paid plan; if so, we will say clearly what costs money before anyone pays, and the free game for kids stays free.

## 2. Who can use it

Anyone can play. Children should have a parent's or guardian's permission. Players whose age answer is under 13 play with everything kept on their device; they cannot post to World boards. Our [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains what is kept and sent.
