Sutra names: how to say them

A listening test for the sutra-pronunciation change. Every Sutra and sub-sutra name from Bharati Krishna Tirtha's 1965 book, in Devanagari and IAST, with a respelling kids can read and clips made on a laptop with the free Kokoro model. Nothing here is in the app yet.

How to read the respelling

Syllables are joined by hyphens. Long names are cut into their parts. One syllable in each part is in CAPITALS: that is where reciters put a light beat today (the second last syllable if it is long or closed, else the one before). It is a small lift, not a strong English stress. What matters more is length: ah, ee, oo, ay, oh are held a little longer, uh, i, u are quick.

uh as in "sofa" · ah as in "father" · i as in "sit" · ee as in "see" · u as in "put" · oo as in "food" · ay as in "say" · oh as in "go" · ai as in "Thai" · ch as in "chair" · sh as in "ship" · dh, bh, kh, th: say the letter with a puff of air (th is never the "th" in "this") · gy for jñ · ri for ṛ.
