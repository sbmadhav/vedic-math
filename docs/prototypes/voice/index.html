Paid voices

Paid cloud voices show up as extra choices above once they are made with that provider's key (GOOGLE_TTS_KEY=... node tools/make-clips.mjs; Azure, OpenAI and ElevenLabs work the same way). The 12 lines here are about 1,500 characters per voice, inside every free tier. Until then, each provider's own demo:

  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: try a Chirp 3 HD voice such as Aoede, Leda or Kore
  • Azure Voice Gallery: Ava, Andrew, Emma (sign-in needed)
  • OpenAI.fm: gpt-4o-mini-tts voices with a style prompt
  • ElevenLabs: Flash v2.5 voices
Every English voice on this device, and how plan A rates it

    Why the paid voice is made ahead of time

    Calling a paid voice every time a line plays costs money every month and sends question text from each Player's device. Making the audio once ahead of time costs a few dollars at most, sends nothing, and still works offline. The numbers are in openspec/changes/better-voice/design.md.
