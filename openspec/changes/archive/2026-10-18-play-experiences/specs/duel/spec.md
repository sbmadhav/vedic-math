# Spec Delta

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Both sides get the same question
**Reason**: The Duel is replaced by Party Mode (play-experiences), where two to four Players race on one screen at their own Mode.
**Migration**: `#/duel` opens `#/arena/party`; the Duelist badge became Party host with the same counter.

### Requirement: Resolving a question
**Reason**: The Duel is replaced by Party Mode (play-experiences).
**Migration**: Party Mode's buzz, lockout and steal rules decide each question.

### Requirement: Pictures are shown in Duels
**Reason**: The Duel is replaced by Party Mode (play-experiences).
**Migration**: Party Mode deals each Player questions at their own Mode.

### Requirement: A Duel ends at 10
**Reason**: The Duel is replaced by Party Mode (play-experiences).
**Migration**: A Party game runs a fixed set of rounds and ends on a podium.
