# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Family helpers hold still
With Calm on, motion reduced on the device or Pause motion on, the Family helpers SHALL appear with a short fade and no movement: no walking, hopping, bobbing or waving. Their words and buttons SHALL be the same. Turning any of the three on while a visit is open SHALL stop its movement at once.

#### Scenario: A visit in Calm
- **WHEN** a Player with Calm on and the helpers on misses a Practice question twice
- **THEN** a friend appears in place with no animation running, and the bubble's words and buttons are the same as without Calm
