# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Family wait for the round's end
In a Star round Bolt SHALL keep teaching as before, and no Family helper SHALL appear while the round runs, whatever happens in it. On the end screen, with the helpers on, a round with 2 or more misses SHALL get a Family visit (at Always), the end screen SHALL offer Ask a friend (at Always or Only when I ask), and lighting a Star on a Skill the friends remember as hard SHALL get their cheer.

#### Scenario: Misses in a Star
- **WHEN** a Player with the helpers on misses two questions in a Star
- **THEN** no friend appears during the round, Bolt replays the Trick as before, and a friend visits once the end screen shows
