Math tricks you can do in your head, and games to practise them

Learn Vedic Math teaches the speed tricks from Bharati Krishna Tirtha's 1965 book Vedic Mathematics, with practice from counting to calculus, games, daily Stars and Boards. Lessons, problems and pictures are our own. Your progress stays on your device.

Where the tricks come from

The sixteen sutras are short rules from Bharati Krishna Tirtha's book, published in 1965. They are not found in the Vedas. We teach them because they are quick and fun, and we name the older Indian mathematicians where their work comes up.

For grown-ups
