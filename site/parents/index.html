For parents

Learn Vedic Math is a free place to learn math, for ages 4 to adult. A child can use it without telling us anything about themselves. Here is what that means in practice.

  • No accounts needed No sign-up and no sign-in, and we never ask for an email. Each Player gets a made-up Nickname.
  • Under 13: nothing is posted online Their scores stay on this device's board, in every Mode.
  • Free, with no ads We never show ads, and we never sell or rent any information.

What stays on your device

Each Player on the device has a made-up Nickname (like "Swift Otter 42", nobody types it), an avatar color, a Mode and the answer to one age question. Progress, the Device board and settings are saved in this browser. We cannot see any of it, and it is not sent to us. You can see and remove it on the game's Me page, or by clearing this site's data in the browser.

Players under 13 post nothing

If a Player's age answer is under 13, the game never posts anything for them online, in any Mode. Their scores rank on this device's board only, and no ID is ever created for them. There is no switch that turns posting on for them.

What leaves the device, and to whom

  • Our host, Cloudflare, receives the device's IP address to deliver the pages, like any website.
  • Two analytics tools, GoatCounter and Cloudflare Web Analytics, count visits and fixed event names without cookies and without any ID. They send nothing when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • A video comes from YouTube (Google) only after a Player presses play. The picture before play comes from our own site.
  • World boards are switched off on this site for now, so today no score is sent anywhere. When they are switched on, Players 13 and older who post to a World board send a made-up Nickname, an avatar color, a score and the time to Google Firebase. Each entry stops being shown after 30 days and is then deleted automatically.

The full list, with the reasons and how long things are kept, is in the privacy policy.

Questions and deletion

Write to privacy@learnvedicmath.com with any question, or to ask us to remove a World board entry (tell us the Nickname, the color and the date). We answer every message.
