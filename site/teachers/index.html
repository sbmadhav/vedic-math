For teachers

Learn Vedic Math is a free game for practising mental math: the speed tricks from Bharati Krishna Tirtha's 1965 book, practice from counting to calculus, games and daily Stars.

A 5-minute warm-up for today's class

Put today's Arena round on the class screen: 60 seconds of quick questions, the same ones for every Player in the same Mode today. The class can race it together on one screen, calling out answers, or each student on their own device. Then talk through one question and the trick behind it.

Open the Arena

The first time on a device, pick the class's Mode. With Calm on, the Arena starts on an untimed round: pick Daily challenge for the 60-second race. There are no class accounts or class leaderboards: each device keeps its own scores.

In a classroom

  • Students use it in a browser on any device, with no accounts and no sign-in. Each device keeps its own progress.
  • Three Modes set the pace and the wording: Explorer (about 4 to 7), Challenger (about 8 to 12) and Pro (13 to adult).
  • Every trick has a short lesson, worked examples and practice that adapts to the student.
  • Students under 13 never post anything online; their scores stay on the device's board.
  • After one visit with a connection, the game works offline.

School plans

There is no school or classroom plan yet, and nothing here is a promise of one. Any plan for a school would need a separate written agreement with that school. If you would like to tell us what would help your class, write to hello@learnvedicmath.com.
